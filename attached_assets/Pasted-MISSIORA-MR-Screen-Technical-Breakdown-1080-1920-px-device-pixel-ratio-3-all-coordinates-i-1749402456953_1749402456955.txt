3 BODY (“camera feed”, 70 %)

A. Hologram avatar
	•	Three.js sprite (512 px² PNG with alpha)
	•	Anchored (position:absolute) at desk center (x:540,y:1080)
	•	WebGL lighting: rim + bloom (strength:1.0, radius:.4)

B. Tamagotchi growth rings
	•	Component <GrowthRings stage="1">
	•	Three SVG concentric circles (radii 88/64/44 px), fill transparent, stroke 6 px
	•	stroke:#8df2e1 for completed, #3a556e for upcoming
	•	Positioned 180 px above avatar head.

C. Orbiting facet icons
	•	3 icons (leaf, target, path) orbit radius 120 px, CSS @keyframes orbit 12s linear infinite.
	•	Each icon also shows tooltip on hover (Identity/Mission/Roadmap).

D. Speech bubble

<SpeechBubble text="Tell me: what lights you up today?"
              width=640
              anchor="center-top"
              coords=(540,530) />

	•	SVG path with 4 px stroke, glass background rgba(59,130,246,.18).

E. Gesture wheel (right)
	•	Column of 4 circular buttons 96 px dia.
	•	Icons (🥕, 💬, 🤝, 🎯); default opacity:.6, hover scale 1.1.
	•	Emits Socket-IO: {gesture:'feed'}, etc.

F. Roadmap pill (top-left inside body)
	•	240×60 px, border-radius:30px, brand gradient stroke.
	•	Text: “Milestone 3 / 7 • Design first offer”.
