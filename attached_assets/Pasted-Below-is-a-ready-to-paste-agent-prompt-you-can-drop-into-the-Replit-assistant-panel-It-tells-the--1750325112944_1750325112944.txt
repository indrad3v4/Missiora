Why instance/ lives outside src/

instance/ holds run-time data (SQLite file, session JSON, secrets).
Per Flask best practice and Uncle Bob’s Clean Architecture, run-time artefacts belong
to the outer-most Frameworks & Drivers ring, not in the compiled source tree.
Placing it beside main.py keeps the code-only src/ tree pure and portable while
still letting the app access it via current_app.instance_path.
